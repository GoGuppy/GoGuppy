Audit and forensics
Who did what, to what, and what it used to be.
📜Policy Change History Who changed which policy and what the old value was. Intune auditEvents, actor plus resource plus modified properties.
🛡️Conditional Access Changes Who changed a CA policy and when. directoryAudits filtered to CA activity, with an old and new value diff.
👤Actor History What did one person do, time bounded. directoryAudits pivoted on initiatedBy.
🎯Target History What changed on one object, time bounded. directoryAudits pivoted on targetResources.
🗓️User Activity Timeline directoryAudits, signIns, Intune auditEvents and the unified audit log merged into one chronological view for a single user. No Microsoft UI does this.
Mail
Rules, forwarding, and the mailboxes nobody is watching.
📥Suspicious Inbox Rules messageRules plus mailboxSettings for one user. Flags forwards, redirects, deletes, and rules hiding mail in unread folders.
📤Consumer Domain Forwarding Anything forwarding to gmail, outlook.com, proton or icloud. Checks rule forwards, forwardingSmtpAddress, and transport rules.
🔓Shared Mailbox Sign In Shared mailboxes with sign in still enabled. Report only, and a standing hole in most tenants.
📈Mailbox Quota Projection Mailboxes approaching quota, with a projected fill date rather than a percentage.
💾Shared Mailboxes Over 50 GB The threshold where a licence is required and nobody notices until mail stops.
Sharing and data exposure
Who outside the tenant can reach what.
🔗Anonymous Link Audit Every anonymous link across every site, with creation and expiry dates, flagging the ones that should already be dead.
🌐External Sharing By Domain Aggregated by outside organisation. Which domains hold access to what.
🖥️Sync Client Inventory Which devices sync which drives, cross referenced against Intune managed devices to surface unmanaged personal machines pulling full drives.
Lifecycle
The offboarding work that usually happens too late.
🕓Pre Termination Share Audit Everything a user shared in the window before their termination date. The investigation nobody runs in time.
📋Offboarding Transfer Manifest What a departing user's drive holds, who it is shared with, and what would move where. Report only, hand the manifest to whoever executes.
Analysis
Questions the portal refuses to answer.
💥Blast Radius Given one user, enumerate the mail, sites, teams, roles, apps and devices they can reach. Answers how bad is it in one shot.
⚔️Policy Conflict Resolver Every setting from every policy assigned to one device, and which policy won each conflict. The portal tells you a conflict exists and then abandons you.
